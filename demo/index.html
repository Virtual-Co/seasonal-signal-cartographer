      About the science

      Paper inspiration: ISBO, arXiv:2610.12213 by Kaichuang Yang, Håvard Rue, and Jakob Zeitler. This independent educational game adds seasonal fog-of-war, a scarce expedition budget, and a seeded random scout. Educational surrogate inspired by ISBO; does not implement INLA, the SPDE mesh, LGCP posterior inference, or the paper's calibrated UCB. Intensity and next-search cues are heuristic illustrations, not a posterior or real-world predictions. Not endorsed by the authors.
